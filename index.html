About Prime Speaking

English Education
Built for Bangladesh

Prime Speaking was founded in 2020 with a clear mission: to make world-class English education accessible to every Bangladeshi learner — regardless of their background or location.

We understand the unique challenges Bangladeshi students face when learning spoken English. Every lesson is designed with Bangla mother-tongue speakers in mind, addressing specific pronunciation issues and building genuine communication confidence.

  • Culturally Relevant — Content that understands the Bangladeshi context and learning style
  • Practically Focused — Every lesson teaches skills you'll actually use in real life
  • Affordable Access — Premium quality at a price accessible to Bangladeshi students everywhere
